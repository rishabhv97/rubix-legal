# Privacy Policy for Rubix Player

**Effective Date:** September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Rubix Player ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
When you register for an account (e.g., as an Admin), we collect:
* **Email Address:** Used for account authentication and securing your watch history.
* **Authentication Data:** Managed securely through our backend provider (Supabase). We do not store your raw passwords.

### B. Usage Data & Watch History
* **Watch History:** We record the videos you watch to provide a seamless "Continue Watching" experience and to maintain your personal watch history. For guest users, this data is stored locally on your device. For logged-in users, this is synced securely to our database.
* **Video Downloads:** The App allows you to download videos for offline viewing. These files are stored locally on your device.

### C. Third-Party Advertising Data
We use **Google AdMob** to display advertisements in the App. Google AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. 
* AdMob may collect device information, IP addresses, and Advertising IDs (such as the Android Advertising ID).
* For more information on how Google uses this data, please visit the [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected data for the following purposes:
* To provide and maintain the App's core functionality (video playback, history, and downloads).
* To manage your account and authentication securely.
* To display relevant advertisements via Google AdMob, which helps keep the App free to use.
* To monitor usage and improve the App's performance and features.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The App utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Below are the links to the privacy policies of the third-party service providers used by the App:
* [Google Play Services Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 4. App Permissions
Depending on your device, the App may request certain permissions to function correctly:
* **Network Access:** Required to stream videos from our servers, log in, and load advertisements.
* **Storage Access:** Required to download and save videos for offline viewing directly to your device.

## 5. Security of Your Data
We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, and we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

## 6. Data Deletion
If you wish to delete your account or any personal data associated with your account, you can log out and clear the App's storage from your Android device settings. Alternatively, you may contact us to request a full account deletion from our backend servers.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
